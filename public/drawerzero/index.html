BCS508 Write-Up

DrawerZero: Project Documentation

1. Problem

Over 60 million tonnes of e-waste are produced annually. Yet 40% of small electronics hoard away in household drawers, risk chemical leakage, and withhold valuable metals like gold, copper, and aluminium from the supply chain.

2. Solution

DrawerZero provides an automated lifecycle audit system. It functions like a milk packet's "use by" date, computing age against standard expected lifespan to prescribe: Keep / Reuse (working and under lifespan), Repair (fixable defects), or Recycle (aged or broken).

3. Tech Stack

HTML5, CSS3 with Flexbox and CSS Grid, JavaScript (ES6+), LocalStorage (key dz_devices), Chart.js (CDN), and Leaflet.js (CDN).

4. Original Feature: Community Drive Threshold

Devices are grouped by 6-digit postal PIN code. When 5 or more devices accumulate in any PIN, an automated community collection drive is scheduled. This resolves the logistical cost and emission barrier of individual transport.

5. Sources & Citations

CPCB E-Waste (Management) Rules 2022, KSPCB Waste Management Cell registers, and UN Global E-Waste Monitor 2024 recovery factors.

6. Benefits

Mitigates heavy metal soil contamination (Pb, Cd, Hg), recovers high-grade urban minerals, and coordinates neighbourhood collective action.
